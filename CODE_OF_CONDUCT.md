# Code of Conduct

Qrive contributors and maintainers commit to a welcoming, harassment-free
community.

Be respectful, discuss ideas rather than people, assume good intent, accept
constructive feedback, and protect other people's privacy. Harassment,
discrimination, threats, doxxing, and publication of real Google Drive data are
not acceptable.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
behavior is harmful or unsafe. Report conduct concerns through
[GitHub Issues](https://github.com/GGULBAE/Qrive/issues) without posting
sensitive personal information. For a concern involving a maintainer, use
GitHub's private contact options when available.
