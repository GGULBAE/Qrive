# Qrive — QR Links for Google Drive

## Summary

Create QR codes locally for files and folders already shared in Google Drive.

## Detailed description

Qrive adds a small QR button beside items that Google Drive already marks as
shared. Select the button to see a QR code for the item's existing sharing
link, copy the link, or save the QR as a PNG.

Qrive is designed around a narrow, privacy-conscious purpose:

- QR codes are created locally in your browser.
- Existing Google Drive sharing permissions remain in control.
- No OAuth, account connection, backend, analytics, advertising, or remote QR
  service is used.
- If Qrive cannot identify a trusted Google Drive link, it shows an error
  instead of creating a potentially incorrect QR code.
- English and Korean labels, keyboard navigation, Escape, outside-click
  dismissal, and focus handling are supported.

A QR code does not make an item public. Anyone who scans it must still satisfy
the item's existing Google Drive access policy.

Qrive runs only on `drive.google.com` and requests no additional Chrome API
permissions. It is open source under the MIT License.

sponsored by Google. Google Drive is a trademark of Google LLC.
